Phase 9 · direction B+ — Dispatch, instrumented

Dispatch, instrumented

Direction B kept its warmth and its restraint, then borrowed the two things Terminal did best — monospace figures and status instrumentation — without the heaviness. Times, dates, windows and counts all read as mono tabular figures. A status LED sits at every row's edge, and a metric ribbon reads the whole book at a glance. The catch C had — solid navy bands that tire the eye — is gone: navy never fills, it survives as a thin spine on cream and as the ink in labels.

Signature — the delivery-window track still leads; the instrumentation stays quiet around it.

Direction B+ keeps B's one risk — a non-text visual in every row, the window track that turns the day's load into a shape you read down the column — and adds Terminal's instrumentation around it without inheriting Terminal's weight. The metric ribbon reads the whole book at a glance and the LED gutter lights each row's status, but both sit on warm cream: navy is a thin spine, never a band. Figures are mono and tabular so times, dates and windows align to the eye. Honours D1–D4; the locked palette is unchanged.
