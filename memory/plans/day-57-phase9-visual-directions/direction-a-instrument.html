Instrument

A precision control surface. Quiet, razor-aligned, near-monochrome — confidence comes from alignment and tabular discipline, not decoration. Navy ink on a cream field; green is withheld until it means something (the one primary action, the one healthy state). Type is an engineered grotesque on a tight scale, with a monospace readout face for labels and figures.

Signature — the status spine: a colour-coded row edge you can read down the whole left margin.

Direction A spends its one risk on restraint: green appears only on the primary action and the healthy state, so the eye learns that green = go. The signature status spine turns the leftmost 3px into a scannable status column — read the rail, not the words. Everything else is navy ink, tabular figures, and hairline structure. Honours D1 (green primary), D2 (sentence case), D3 (two-column fill), D4 (humanised phone, "consignee", names not hashes).
