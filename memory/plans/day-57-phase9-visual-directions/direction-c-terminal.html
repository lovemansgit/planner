A logistics operations terminal. Maximum density, monospace tabular numerics, a navy structural band reading the whole book at a glance — the table is the interface, the way a trading or dispatch desk works. Cool cream keeps it from going cold. Labels stay sentence-case (the locked rule); the terminal feel comes from density and mono figures, never from shouting.

Direction C spends its one risk on density at the edge: 38px rows, monospace tabular numerics, and a navy band that doubles as a live metric ribbon. The status-LED gutter is an indicator light per row — the desk reads colour first. To stay a terminal and not a newspaper, it leans on colour-keyed bands, zebra fills, and the ribbon — not bare hairlines — and it keeps D2 sentence-case rather than shouting in caps. Honours D1–D4.
